# First Slice: Effect and Handler Rules

This working trace refines E1–E6 in the [rule catalogue](first-slice-catalogue-and-map.md#effect-handler-and-control-rules) and V1–V2 in the [verification audit](first-slice-verification-audit.md#requests-and-handlers). The [specification](../../../spec/README.md) states current intended semantics. The code links identify present checks and execution paths; they do not prove correspondence. The required judgments below are design requirements, not implemented rules or a choice of representation. Continuation use, discard and resource completion have separate obligations; see the [continuation trace](first-slice-continuation-use-trace.md) and [judgment requirements](first-slice-judgment-requirements.md).

A certified constituent can be conditional on its premises. A new composition must establish those premises or preserve them explicitly; it must not inspect or verify a previously certified body, clause, operand, function or module again. Ordinary function definition and application are outside this slice. A call supplies its established judgment, including any relevant resource and control facts; its row alone is not known to provide them. Abstract types and differently named type parameters do not prove independence or inequality. Surface inference may establish facts that typed Core must carry; this is a requirement for the intended account, not a claim that current Core already carries all of them. Root allocation and excluded interpreter, resource and external contracts remain boundary premises. An outstanding premise is not a proof.

## E1 — Effect declaration

**Specification and execution.** An [effect declaration](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#effects) has an ordered parameter vector with admitted kinds other than `EffectRow`, a nonempty ordered operation vector with unique spellings, and a pure outer `P -> Q` for each operation. Operation types are formed under the effect parameters. Defining module and declaration order give the `EffectId`; operation owner and order give the `OperationId`. Equal names or signatures from different declarations do not identify them. Formation has no operational step. Its identities and signatures are instantiated for E4 and compared during E6.

**Current path.** [Source identity installation](../../../src/resolve.rs#L3117) assigns IDs from module and ordinal; [declaration resolution](../../../src/resolve.rs#L3275) resolves parameter-scoped operation types and rejects a non-pure outer arrow at [the shape check](../../../src/resolve.rs#L3442). The [Core registry validator](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L8054) takes registry, module/source mapping and parameter owners; it checks provenance, order, nonempty operations, admitted and uniquely owned parameters, unique operation IDs and spellings, function shape, kinds and free parameters. Invalid registry facts reject; resource exhaustion is a separate failure. The registry supplies [operation signature instantiation](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L13935), not runtime code for a declaration.

**Facts and judgment needed.** A checked declaration introduces authenticated identities, ordered kinds and operation contracts under their parameter assumptions; it does not establish any handler, implementation or runtime interpreter. Instantiation must preserve identity and substitute all arguments consistently. The declaration judgment must be reusable across imports and certified request and handler compositions without reopening its source. It cannot turn equal spelling into identity or promise a matching handler. Whether and how the existing summaries and Core authentication expose the required reusable certification is open.

## E2 — Exact instance and row formation

**Specification and execution.** [WF-Effect-Instance, WF-Closed-Effect-Row, WF-Open-Effect-Row and WF-Computation](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#computation-types-and-effect-rows) require a known declaration, complete ordered kind-correct arguments, distinct written exact members, an `EffectRow` tail with known members absent from it, and a value type of kind `Type`. Instance identity includes the effect and all zonked arguments. Written duplicates reject even though inferred sets coalesce. Open rows carry lacks facts. Formation itself has no execution step: retained exact instances and their lexical substitutions later govern dispatch.

**Current path.** [Source row resolution](../../../src/resolve.rs#L7052) resolves effect names and arguments, records arity and constructs the tail and provenance; [source row construction](../../../src/resolve.rs#L88) detects duplicate members. The [instance resolver](../../../src/resolve.rs#L7226) resolves a complete target. Core [validates instances and rows](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L14042) against its registry and binder kinds, rejecting invalid owner arguments or rows; it also checks registry and type formation separately. An operation use additionally authenticates its owner and instantiated signature in [effect_signature](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L13935). The rows in Core nodes are retained by [effect lowering](../../../spec/elaboration.md#effect-core-lowering).

**Facts and judgment needed.** Formation introduces well-kinded exact identity and the stated row membership and lacks facts, not an interpreter or safety of a request. It preserves argument identity and assumptions, and excludes duplicate written membership. A reusable judgment must expose the declaration contract, complete arguments, kind environment and row constraints through substitution and module boundaries. A tail not known to contain or lack an instance cannot be treated as doing either. Parameters that differ syntactically may become equal after specialization; no judgment may use their names as proof of permanent inequality. The present links do not establish complete source/Core correspondence for every formation error.

## E3 — Equality, substitution and row operations

**Specification and execution.** The [row algebra](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#computation-types-and-effect-rows) compares known sets and, for open rows, tail identity and accumulated lacks after substitution. Kind-preserving substitution reaches all arguments and preserves lacks; occurs or forbidden replacement fails. Unification aligns exact identities and unifies arguments without inventing lacks. Partial union coalesces known equality but rejects incompatible unknown tails or an added member not known absent from a retained tail. Weakening checks against an expected row; it does not alter synthesized least effects. Exact removal requires an explicitly established target; absent closed membership is missing and unknown-tail membership is ambiguous. The separate hook discharge relation is not ordinary handler admission. These static operations have no direct transition; runtime instantiation and exact dispatch consume their resulting identities.

**Current path.** Source [unifies effect rows](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L10083), [unions effects](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L13845), and zonks rows and arguments at [row zonking](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L9674). Shared [row union](../../../src/types.rs#L884) takes two rows and rejects distinct tails or missing exclusions; [exact removal](../../../src/types.rs#L969) returns a remainder, `Ambiguous` or `Missing`. [Row substitution](../../../src/types.rs#L1152) and type substitution preserve the corresponding constraints or report errors. Core validates final rows and uses the same row operations to check node equations, including [handler removal](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L8914); it does not prove a general correspondence merely by using the same helpers.

**Facts and judgment needed.** A successful operation introduces its resulting equality, union, inclusion or remainder relationship; removal discharges only the exact established member, preserving all other members and applicable lacks. Substitution preserves constraints under its stated well-kinded substitution; it must re-evaluate equality after parameters coincide. A compositional judgment needs the actual substitution, equality and existing lacks evidence, not a fresh assumption that a tail excludes a member. Unsolved relationships remain obligations or fail according to the applicable current rule. The precise reusable type-level account and per-consumer disposition of source and Core row work remain open.

## E4 — Operation request

**Specification and execution.** [T-Perform](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#effects) substitutes the exact instance into the operation contract, checks the operand against `P`, and yields `Q` with operand effects union the exact request. Ambiguous union rejects unless an existing expected-row lacks fact establishes it. It does not select a provider. [E-Perform-Operand](../../../spec/dynamic-semantics.md#effect-handlers-and-completion) saves lexical instantiation and evaluates the operand before selection; operand effects occur before the request.

**Current path.** [Source signature lookup and request elaboration](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L13919) authenticate the operation and substitute instance arguments, check the operand, then [finish_perform](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L13999) unions its effects and creates a typed Core `Request`; mismatched types or ambiguous union fail. The [Core request check](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L15806) takes the retained instance, operation, operand node and node type/row; it checks ownership, substituted domain and result, exact operand type, and equality to the operand-row/singleton union. Invalid identity, argument, result or row rejects. At execution, [request entry](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L5715) saves the environment's instantiation and evaluates the operand, and [return_request](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L4440) instantiates and searches only after it returns.

**Facts and judgment needed.** The new computation introduces the possible exact request and result type, and preserves operand effects and relevant facts about the actual payload, dependencies and pending control. It does not eliminate an effect or establish selection. The rule must compose the certified operand and instantiated operation contract, including the union premise, without revisiting either. The payload's ordinary `P` type alone need not describe its actual dependencies; opaque call and value judgments must supply them or leave an obligation. A request's row alone also does not establish what control it can capture. These facts matter to the separate ownership and completion rules and to excluding an unhandled or invalidly interpreted request under the full program's premises; this local judgment alone cannot guarantee an interpreter.

## E5 — Handler admission and normal return

**Specification and execution.** [T-Handle](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#effects) requires the exact target explicitly in the body row, one clause per operation, irrefutable patterns at the substituted domains, a shared answer type and each clause checked under its fresh `resume(Q, A, epsilon_out)` capability. It removes only the body's exact target and chooses the least output row containing the remainder and direct clause effects. A direct same-instance clause request remains in that row. [E-Handle-Enter and E-Handle-Return](../../../spec/dynamic-semantics.md#effect-handlers-and-completion) install the boundary and remove it on normal body return, passing the value outward; there is no return clause.

**Current path.** [Source finish_handler](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L14550) removes the target from the body row, checks clauses and constructs the Core handler; [check_effect_clauses](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L14798) checks operation coverage and clause contracts. Missing or ambiguous removal and invalid coverage, pattern, type or clause usage reject. The [Core handler check](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L16777) takes the body, exact instance, clauses, registry and node contract; it validates a unique handler ID, instance, coverage, clause identities and capabilities, body answer and remainder inclusion, clause effects, and the [least output](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L16414). [Pattern and answer validation](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L17025) checks domain, irrefutability and shared answer; failures reject. Core's independently reconstructed checks are current behavior, not the intended no-repeat account. The evaluator [enters the body under a handler frame](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L5728) and [pops a returning boundary](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L3519).

**Facts and judgment needed.** Handler formation introduces a local capability and clause bindings under their stated assumptions, removes the exact body requirement, and preserves other requirements and direct clause effects. It neither removes a same-instance request made directly by a clause nor statically chooses which dynamic boundary receives a request. The new composition must establish coverage, pattern, shared answer and least-row premises from certified body, clauses and declaration; discharge only local assumptions justified by the rule. A clause can be certified conditionally on its capability and capture assumptions, with the handler composition establishing or retaining the applicable premises. It must account for normal return, one-shot use and relevant captured/outside owners without opening either constituent. The continuation and resource rules supply additional premises; admission and row equality alone cannot prove completion or safe discard.

## E6 — Nearest interpretation and capture

**Specification and execution.** After operand evaluation, [E-Request-Select](../../../spec/dynamic-semantics.md#effect-handlers-and-completion) instantiates the request using its saved lexical context and searches outward for the nearest accepting source, cell or native boundary. The displayed source branch requires exact identity and arguments, its matching clause and successful irrefutable match, with no nearer accepting cell or native boundary. It moves the selected handler and intervening frames into `live(captured)` owned by the clause, then executes the clause under the handler's saved environment and the untouched outer continuation. The selected handler is no longer active around that clause; row order is irrelevant. Cell and native boundaries use their own execution steps.

**Current path.** [return_request](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L4440) instantiates the request, scans frames from inner to outer and tests native, resource and source candidates in order; source matching instantiates the handler in its own environment and compares the exact instance. It returns `ForeignBinding` if no candidate is found. [Clause selection and capture](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L4657) find the operation clause, match its pattern, move the segment and install the clause boundary; malformed control or impossible match fails. The [capture analysis](../../../src/core/capture/control.rs#L1) models possible nearest-handler paths for control obligations, while [Core handler validation](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L16777) checks structural contracts. Neither a row membership check nor handler coverage itself selects a runtime boundary. This mapping locates the implementation; it does not establish all-path equivalence to the operational semantics.

**Facts and judgment needed.** Selection introduces an available clause owner with its exact selected capture and preserves its ordered frames, saved instantiations, payload facts and untouched outside obligations. It removes the selected boundary and intervening frames from active clause control, not from the owned capture. Nested live captures and completion owners must be related to their actual owning context; they are not inferred from effect names. A compositional control judgment must describe possible requests and their contextual capture obligations through opaque calls and frames, permit specialization where parameters coincide, and connect the certified handler and request contracts to the actual nearest boundary. If required facts about a callee or outside control are missing, the affected conclusion remains conditional or the relevant composition rejects under current rules; uncertainty alone does not reject resource-free resume-only code. Direct clause requests use the outside context, so an outer same-instance handler is a possible later interpreter, not the selected boundary still active around the clause.

## Correctness and remaining work

These traces identify premises relevant to preventing wrong operation signatures, missing clauses, impossible pattern matches, incorrect exact dispatch and lost control or ownership facts. A complete accepted program also needs the other slice rules, declared boundary and environment premises, and separate system-level soundness and execution correspondence. Current runtime failure paths and resource/work limits need their own audit under the narrow guarantee; this record does not classify every failure as external or claim them unreachable.

The representation of reusable declaration, row, payload and control judgments, their preservation through source inference and typed Core, and the exact disposition of every current consumer remain open. In particular, [AD-0007](../../decisions/AD-0007-typed-core-dialects.md) requires independent Core validation today; reconciling it with certified-constituent reuse is required before replacing checks. These links and checks are bounded evidence, not a proof or an implementation proposal.

Representative tests inspected, not run, cover [identity and open-row lacks](../../../tests/effect_rows.rs#L17), [exact removal and direct clause requests](../../../tests/effect_typing.rs#L507), and [generic request dispatch](../../../tests/effect_runtime.rs#L289). They do not prove completeness or correspondence.
