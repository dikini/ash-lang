# First Slice: Cleanup and Closing

This working trace follows R7–R10 in the [rule catalogue](first-slice-catalogue-and-map.md#rule-catalogue), V5 and V10–V12 in the [verification audit](first-slice-verification-audit.md#cleanup-and-rows), and the separate [discard ownership trace](first-slice-discard-ownership-trace.md). It identifies the current checks and the facts a candidate composition must use. The candidate names below are not Ash syntax, a complete calculus, or a claim that the current checks are redundant. The [specification](../../../spec/README.md) is the authority for current behavior.

## R7 — Scope and invocation obligation

**Current rule.** The [static cleanup rule](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#scoped-cleanup-actions) checks the action as `Unit -> Unit ! epsilon_cleanup`. Anonymous functions receive the `Unit` domain and result context while their latent row is inferred; ordinary callbacks, imported factories and generic functions use the same rules, without a universal purity or closed-row requirement. The result has the body's value type; its least outward row includes initializer evaluation, body effects and the action's latent effects. A handler confined to the body cannot discharge the later action; a handler inside the action can. Callback shape and missing effects use ordinary type and row diagnostics. The action is evaluated once in the preceding environment. Only successful preparation acquires one invocation obligation; copies of the callable acquire none. See [cleanup execution](../../../spec/dynamic-semantics.md#scoped-cleanup-actions).

**Current path.** [Action binding](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L16985) checks an initializer against a function from `Unit` to `Unit` with a fresh latent row. [Action completion](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L17021) unifies an inference tail with accumulated inferred effects, or checks that accumulated effects fit an established expected row; ambiguous inference and outside effects fail with row diagnostics. [Scope scheduling](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L16963) checks the callback type and schedules the action and body. [Scope completion](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L16882) takes the body type, unions body and invocation effects, and builds the cleanup node; its surrounding binding accounts for initializer evaluation. It also carries or defers the selected allocation context when necessary. [Core owner validation](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L13251) checks that the invocation applies the same callback to `Unit`, has the right function domain and result, and that the cleanup node agrees with body type and unioned body/action effects. Invalid shape, identity, type, row or origin rejects Core. It does not itself establish all control and resource relationships. At runtime [acquisition](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L3719) looks up a callable and reserves capacity before publishing the boundary and entering the body; preparation can fail without publishing an owner.

**Candidate composition.** From certified initializer, callable and body judgments, establish the body result type and the union of their applicable effects. The initializer's certified value must identify the callable and its established `Unit -> Unit` contract, actual dependency and control facts, instantiation and assumptions. Successful acquisition introduces a fresh invocation owner associated with that callable and its selected allocation context, initially armed; it is distinct from the callable's value identity. The initializer and its effects precede that introduction. The rule preserves the body's result dependencies and relevant outside obligations; it does not claim that the owner has already run. It must establish ordinary row-union and type-instantiation premises, and retain an explicit obligation where the opaque callable's needed facts are unavailable.

## R8 — Phase and completion

**Current rule.** [Cleanup execution](../../../spec/dynamic-semantics.md#scoped-cleanup-actions) defines initialization, acquisition, start and finish. Normal return saves the body value, starts the armed action with `()`, ignores its `Unit` result and returns the saved value unchanged after completion. An admitted owned close instead starts an armed action and advances to the next owner after it returns. Owners close in structural inner-to-outer order. Capture and resume preserve the current phase and saved value; start consumes eligibility to start again but leaves completion required. A running action or unfinished close cannot be abandoned. An action-local discard may succeed when the action, close and outside owners remain able to complete. Outward requests can capture and resume running cleanup. These are successful-transition conditions, not termination or native-release guarantees.

**Current path.** In [close traversal](../../../src/core/capture/control.rs#L7032), the analysis takes a selected capture, discard event and graph state. In strict mode it rejects a running cleanup, unfinished close or retained private root in the owned segment, marks an armed cleanup for closing, and treats captured source handlers as removed. The ownership and outside-reachability checks are separately traced in the [discard trace](first-slice-discard-ownership-trace.md#current-checks). [Runtime boundary and completion frames](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L3374) save the returned body value before invoking the action, then require `Unit`, decrement the live-owner count and restore that value. [Discard preparation](../../../src/eval/machine.rs#L5254) first authenticates the selected live owner and prepares the owned transfer. Internal failure can occur during preparation or after actions have started; later failure neither rolls back effects nor promises remaining actions will run. It is not an automatically external event. The [runtime failure rules](../../../spec/dynamic-semantics.md#observation) also classify capacity exhaustion as internal.

**Candidate composition.** Carry a fact for each particular owner and activation: armed, running with its saved normal value or close continuation, or finished. Capture and resume preserve that fact and its dependencies. A normal-return rule changes armed to running while saving the actual result; a successful finish discharges that owner and restores that same result. An owned close consumes eligibility to start each armed action in structural order but does not claim completion before successful execution. For an action-local discard, the running action may be outside the owned closure: the rule must prove its completion remains reachable, rather than rejecting solely because some action is running. An owner outside the closure is not thereby proved safe; its continuation and other outside obligations must survive. Conversely, an owned running action or unfinished close cannot be skipped. These phase and reachability facts must compose with the separately established ownership, capability-use, interpreter and row premises.

## R9 — Interpreter at a structural closing position

**Current rule.** The [static cleanup rules](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#scoped-cleanup-actions) require compatibility for each actual transitive request of an action at its structural closing position. Lookup accounts for the action's own handlers, still-enclosing captured boundaries and surviving discard context with their lexical instantiations and precedence. A body-local handler already passed at that position is excluded. Captured source and generated hook handlers are candidates for this proof, not restored executable handlers. If the nearest matching candidate is removed, abandonment fails with `effect.cleanup-context`; an outer same-instance handler or builtin cell cannot substitute. Action-local handlers, surviving outer handlers and live unshadowed builtin cells have their specified roles. Unknown necessary interpreter requirements reject the relevant abandonment, not all ordinary callback uses. Imported and generic facts and open rows retain their lexical authority.

**Current path.** The shared control graph discovers possible requests, selected handlers and callback relationships using the current code and imported summaries; see [control discovery](../../../src/core/capture/control.rs#L5768). [Removed-handler checking](../../../src/core/capture/control.rs#L6237) takes the selected request and its path; in strict checking it reports `CleanupContext` with discard, action and handler locations when that selection is a removed handler. During ordinary cleanup-row inference without actual-capture authentication it instead continues, so this inference result is not proof of interpreter safety. [Source capture checking](../../../src/elaborate.rs#L5818) runs after row solving, using globals, imported capture summaries, method routes and adapters. [Linked Core validation](../../../src/core/validate.rs#L13848) reconstructs capture facts when its resource, generic-cell or discard gate applies and maps a removed-interpreter failure to a Core validation cause. These paths use related machinery, but an overlapping graph does not establish that each run verifies the same subject and premises.

**Candidate composition.** An action's certified contract must provide its relevant possible transitive request and control relationships, including exact instance arguments, lexical instantiation, local interpretation and any conditional opaque-call assumptions. A closing composition supplies the selected capture, its structural position, removed and retained boundaries, surviving context and actual instantiation. It must establish the nearest applicable candidate for every relevant possible action request and show that candidate remains usable. The relationship is contextual: an outward row alone neither identifies the nearest candidate nor proves that an outer same-instance boundary can replace a removed one. Where the needed request or selection fact is unavailable, retain the obligation or reject unconditional abandonment. The account must also preserve request payload and reply dependencies across the selected boundaries; interpreter compatibility does not by itself prove safe payload crossing.

## R10 — Residual row and dependencies

**Current rule.** [T-Discard](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#effects) consumes the available ordinary capability, requires the owned closure and preserved outside/running completion, compatible interpreters and the least residual row, and returns `Unit` with that row. The close row includes the latent requirements of owned actions after action-local and still-enclosing retained builtin cell interpretation. Surviving source handlers, including clause-local handlers, discharge residual requirements at their own boundaries. [Cleanup dependency rules](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#scoped-cleanup-actions) apply to the saved body value, action captures and operation payloads. Access to an enclosing live cell is allowed but does not authorize exporting its dependencies; escaping dependencies produce `type.resource-escape`. [Resource crossing](../../../spec/static-semantics.md#scoped-cells-and-resource-dependencies) also admits the owner's trusted interpreter as a recipient. The payload's ordinary type alone does not prove independence.

**Current path.** [Requirement recording](../../../src/core/capture/control.rs#L6855) substitutes each action's latent row at its actual lexical context, finds still-enclosing resource interpreters and the discard's context, and records action row, retained exact instances and expected discard row per relevant request context. [Source solving](../../../src/elaborate/cleanup_rows.rs#L392) consumes those requirements plus pending equations. It initializes pending solutions to empty, iterates finite monotone unions and exact removals to a fixed point, then solves rows, checks equalities and revisits deferred escapes; work exhaustion publishes no solution. [Strict row checking](../../../src/core/capture.rs#L3871) separately groups requirements by discard code and context, removes exact retained instances, unions action rows and compares with the expected row. A discard with no local lexical requirement must have an empty annotated row. Mismatch yields a cleanup-row failure; malformed row operations or exhausted work fail as well. [Value dependency analysis](../../../src/core/capture/value.rs#L5929) and [control/payload checks](../../../src/core/capture/control.rs#L7587) supply separate escape and crossing results. Core validation runs the shared analysis under its gate and rejects unsafe, context, row or dependency failures; it does not accept an empty row annotation as proof.

**Candidate composition.** From the certified owned close plan and each action's instantiated latent row, compose the least residual row under the action-local and retained builtin interpretation applicable at each structural position. Use the row contract, not a guessed enumeration of only requests observed on one execution. Source handler compositions discharge appropriate residual effects separately. In parallel, propagate dependencies of the particular saved result, action closure and each possible payload and reply, and establish independence or authorized crossing at each owner boundary. A saved result is restored only after successful normal completion; in a discard the abandoned body has no invented result. The relevant cell owner can remain live during an inner action but its captured access cannot escape on that account. An unsolved row, unknown relevant dependency, or missing crossing premise is an obligation, not an independence proof.

## Reuse, evidence and open work

At an opaque boundary, a function, callback, value or outside frame supplies its established judgment and assumptions; the new cleanup, capture, handler or discard composition checks its new relationships against those facts. It must not inspect or verify the constituent again to recover missing information. Instantiation must preserve exact identities, possible parameter coincidence, row constraints and dependencies. Existing imported summaries provide some reuse, but current source and linked Core also reconstruct relationships. The eventual design must map subjects, contexts and consumers before changing any check, and reconcile certified reuse with [AD-0007](../../decisions/AD-0007-typed-core-dialects.md). Shared graph consumers outside this slice still need their own disposition.

Representative tests inspected, not run: [body-local handlers, armed/running and action-local discard](../../../tests/cleanup_typing.rs#L85); [capture/resume and saved body result](../../../tests/cleanup_typing.rs#L200); [escaping action payload](../../../tests/cleanup_typing.rs#L266); [removed handler versus passed body-local handler](../../../tests/cleanup_abandonment.rs#L302); [opaque action and imported boundary](../../../tests/cleanup_abandonment.rs#L83); and [distinct recursive cleanup activations](../../../tests/cleanup_interactions.rs#L69). They demonstrate selected cases, not completeness or proof of safety.

The remaining design must derive phase and outside-completion facts from every relevant certified capture; express actual transitive request selection and lexical context through opaque and polymorphic calls; prove the row equations and retained-interpreter treatment for every applicable context; and compose saved-value, capture, payload, backing-storage and external assumptions across all relevant boundaries. Region, registration, Store and native rules remain outside the slice, but their specific supplied facts and obligations cannot be discarded. The agreed [failure admission rule](first-slice-failure-judgments.md#agreed-admission-rule-for-the-first-slice) requires internal capacity and work failures to be ruled out for the no-error conclusion; their individual guards and evidence remain work for the slice. This trace does not establish that current checking rules out every in-scope execution failure, that failures are external, or that any individual existing check can be removed. System-level justification against the operational rules remains separate from checking a program.
